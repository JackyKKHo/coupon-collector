Drop rate: accepts any of these — 3000 (= 1 in 3000), 1 in 3000, 1/3000, 0.0003, or 0.03%.
Qty needed: how many copies of that item you need to collect.
Count: how many separate items share this exact rate and qty needed (e.g. a clue casket table with 15 different uniques all at 1/360) — leave at 1 for a single item.
Expected Value shows the mean number of trials. Distribution shows the quantile curve — switch between By probability % (trials needed for a given chance) and By trials / KC (chance of being done at a trial count you enter).
Kills per hour: optional — fill it in to convert trial counts into expected playtime.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.
